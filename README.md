# 🌿 AyuSetu
### Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal for Skill Mapping, Internships and Placement in the Ayush Ecosystem

> **Ministry of Ayush | All India Institute of Ayurveda**  
> Category: Software | Theme: Smart Automation

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Students graduating from Ayush institutions (BAMS, BUMS, BHMS, BNYS, Siddha) face a fundamental disconnect:

- They don't know **which skills industries actually want**
- They have no clear way to **measure their own skill gaps**
- Generic job portals like Naukri or Internshala have **zero understanding of Ayush-specific competencies** — they don't know what Panchakarma or Nadi Pariksha means
- Industries struggle to **find and verify** candidates with the right domain skills
- Institutions have **no visibility** into whether their students are placement-ready
- Academicians have **no dedicated channel** for FDPs, faculty internships, or collaborative research in the Ayush space

There is no single platform built specifically for the Ayush ecosystem that connects all these stakeholders.

---

## 💡 What We Are Building

**AyuSetu** is a unified, role-based collaboration portal built exclusively for the Ayush ecosystem.

It connects three groups of people on a single platform — **students**, **industry partners**, and **institutions** — through a shared, domain-specific Ayush skill vocabulary.

The platform is not a generic job portal. It is built around the idea that **skills must be measured, gaps must be identified, and matches must be explainable** — all using terminology and competency frameworks that actually belong to the Ayush domain.

---

## 👥 Who Uses It

### 🎓 Students
Ayush students (BAMS, BUMS, BHMS, BNYS, Siddha) who want to:
- Understand where they stand skill-wise
- Find internships, jobs, and training programs that match their profile
- Build a verified digital portfolio of their skills and achievements
- Track every application they've made in one place

### 🏭 Industry & Academia Partners
Companies, hospitals, research organisations, and academic institutions that want to:
- Post internships, jobs, apprenticeships, FDPs, and training programs
- Find candidates whose skills actually match what the role needs
- Issue verified digital certificates to successful candidates
- Collaborate through mentorship, live projects, and workshops

### 🏫 Institution Admins
College administrators and department heads who want to:
- See the overall skill health of their student cohort
- Identify which skills are most commonly missing across students
- Track how many students are progressing through the placement pipeline
- Make informed decisions about curriculum and training priorities

## 🔄 How It Works — The Core Loop

**Step 1 — Student signs up and completes a skill assessment**  
The assessment is built around Ayush-specific competency domains — not generic tech skills. Students rate their proficiency across clinical, pharmaceutical, research, digital, and professional skill areas.

**Step 2 — A skill profile is generated**  
The platform produces a visual radar chart showing the student's strengths and weaknesses across the five Ayush competency domains.

**Step 3 — Skill gaps are identified**  
The platform compares the student's profile against what industries are actually looking for and highlights the gaps in a clear, ranked format.

**Step 4 — Ranked opportunity matches appear**  
Internships, jobs, FDPs, and training programs are ranked by how well they match the student's current skill profile. Each match comes with a plain-language explanation — which skills matched, which are missing, whether the student is eligible.

**Step 5 — Student applies and tracks status**  
Students apply directly through the platform and can see their application status in real time — Applied, Shortlisted, Interview, Selected, or Rejected.

**Step 6 — Industry reviews and shortlists**  
Industry partners see a ranked list of applicants with matched and missing skills highlighted. They can update the status of each applicant.

**Step 7 — Certificate is issued**  
After a successful internship or program, the industry partner issues a digital certificate on the platform. This certificate has a unique verification ID.

**Step 8 — Portfolio goes public**  
The certificate appears on the student's public portfolio, which can be shared with anyone and independently verified using the verification ID.

## 🌿 The Key Differentiator — Ayush Skill Taxonomy

The core of AyuSetu is an **Ayush-specific skill vocabulary** organised into five domains:

| Domain | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **Clinical & Therapeutic** | Panchakarma, Nadi Pariksha, Prakriti analysis, Yoga therapy, Marma therapy |
| **Pharmaceutical & Manufacturing** | Bhaishajya Kalpana, GMP compliance, Herbal extraction, Quality control |
| **Research & Data** | Clinical trial design, Biostatistics, Samhita literature research, CTRI registration |
| **Digital & Emerging** | Ayush teleconsultation, ABDM standards, NAMASTE coding, EMR systems |
| **Professional** | Patient counselling, Medical ethics, NABH readiness, Wellness entrepreneurship |

No generic job portal understands these skills. AyuSetu is built around them.

| Login / Signup | Role-based registration for all user types |

| Public Portfolio | Shareable student page with verified skills and certificates |
| Certificate Verification | Anyone can verify a certificate using its public ID |
| Industry Dashboard | Overview of posted opportunities and applicant counts |
| Post Opportunity | Form for industry to post an internship or job with required skills |
| Applicant View | Ranked list of applicants with skill match details and status controls |
| Institution Dashboard | Cohort analytics — skill health, top gaps, placement funnel |
